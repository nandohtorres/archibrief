What it checks

16 deterministic rules cover the common, provable misconfigurations. The grounded review then weighs them against your brief.

Public exposure

Buckets, security groups, and subnets that are reachable from the internet.

S3 bucket has no public-access block

Identity & access

Wildcard permissions and trust policies without conditions.

IAM policy grants Action "*"

Data protection

Storage without encryption at rest, versioning, or point-in-time recovery.

RDS instance lacks storage_encrypted

Resilience

Single points of failure measured against the availability your brief asks for.

RDS instance is not multi_az

Audit & logging

Whether activity would leave a durable, tamper-evident trail.

CloudTrail log validation disabled

Brief vs. code

Requirements in your brief that the submitted code doesn't appear to meet, or can't show.

"99.95% availability" vs. one AZ

The honest fine print

Your AWS account stays out of it

No credentials are requested and no calls are made into your environment. Only the files you submit are read.

Bounded by what you submit

Controls set elsewhere, like an organization-wide trail or account-level public-access block, can't be seen and are flagged as such.

A second opinion, not an audit

It doesn't certify compliance or guarantee safety. It gives you cited findings to make a better decision.
