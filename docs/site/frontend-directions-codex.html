    Exposure and access

    High riskBucket-level public-access protection is not declared›

    Why it matters

    The submitted Terraform does not enable S3 Block Public Access at the bucket level. For a bucket intended to hold transaction audit trails, unintended exposure would undermine confidentiality and the integrity of the evidence trail.

    Recommended action

    Enable S3 Block Public Access at both the bucket and account levels, then validate the effective bucket policy.

    Confidence: High. A deterministic rule found the relevant Terraform setting directly. Confidence describes evidence strength, not a guarantee that no runtime exception exists.

    Why it matters

    Recommended action

    Limit the policy to the required S3 actions and the specific bucket resources, then refine it against observed usage.
